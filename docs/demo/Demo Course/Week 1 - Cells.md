# Week 1: Cells (demo course notes)

These notes are made-up sample material for the Shortcut demo.

## Organelles and what they do

| Organelle | Function | Found in |
|---|---|---|
| Nucleus | Stores the cell's DNA and controls its activity | Plant and animal cells |
| Ribosome | Protein synthesis; ribosomes have **no** membrane | All cells |
| Mitochondrion | Main site of aerobic respiration ("powerhouse of the cell") | Plant and animal cells |
| Chloroplast | Photosynthesis | Plant cells only |
| Cell wall | Supports the cell | Plant cells, not animal cells |
| Golgi apparatus | Packages and ships proteins | Plant and animal cells |

## Levels of organisation

In this course we always list the levels from smallest to largest:
atom → molecule → cell → tissue → organ.

## Growth of a culture

A population that doubles every *d* minutes grows to N = N₀ × 2^(t/d).
Example used in class: a culture that doubles every 30 minutes.
