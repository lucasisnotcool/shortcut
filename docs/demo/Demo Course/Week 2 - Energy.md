# Week 2: Energy in cells (demo course notes)

These notes are made-up sample material for the Shortcut demo.

## Photosynthesis

carbon dioxide + water → (light) → **glucose** + **oxygen**

It takes place in the chloroplast.

## Respiration

glucose + oxygen → carbon dioxide + water + energy (ATP)

Aerobic respiration mostly happens in the mitochondria.

## Exam tip

Remember: the products of photosynthesis are the reactants of respiration.
